## workshop

Final asset: `public/static/images/studio/workshop.webp`

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: original hero illustration for Coooder's personal engineering blog, not a website screenshot.
Primary request: a richly hand-drawn independent sci-fi comic illustration of a young adult developer at a makeshift retro computer workbench, with a small friendly robot assistant.
Scene: a lived-in compact workshop with a chunky CRT terminal, patch cables, notebook, keyboard and one small potted plant. A curious developer with tousled dark hair, round glasses and a rust-red work jacket leans over the desk, working alongside the little sage-green robot.
Style: confident imperfect black ink contours, visible pencil construction marks, crosshatching and flat gouache washes, handmade alternative comic/risograph editorial print. Detailed retro-tech illustration with expressive character, not polished vector and absolutely not 3D.
Composition: near-square portrait-friendly composition, characters large and central, three-quarter waist-up view and desk foreground. Readable strong silhouettes. Generous warm cream margin at upper edges, objects contained in frame. No lettering; website copy will be outside image.
Palette: warm paper #F1EBDD, charcoal #27252A, muted moss #687A59, brick red #B95F48, occasional dusty pink and pale mint. Restrained, warm, textured.
Constraints: original character and composition; no logos, text, lettering, watermarks, UI elements or brand marks. No glossy render, neon lighting or gradients. References are aesthetic direction only: retro-tech ink illustration and expressive loose hand-painted sci-fi comics.
```
